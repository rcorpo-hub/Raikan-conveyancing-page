FAQs

Good to know before you start

What does a conveyancer do?

A conveyancer prepares transfer documents and helps handle the steps involved in changing property ownership. Read more at SA.GOV.AU.

When should I contact a conveyancer?

Get in touch while planning a purchase, sale or transfer. If you have questions about the contract, speak to a conveyancer before signing.

What if I’m unsure of my purchase price?

Choose “I’m not sure yet” in the buying quote form. You can start the enquiry while you’re still looking.
